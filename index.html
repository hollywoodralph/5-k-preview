COURSE ROUTE

Scenic Waterfront Route Along San Diego Bay

Swipe
1

The course begins at the North Embarcadero Marina and follows the marina walkway counterclockwise before heading south (right turn) continuing down along the Bayfront Promenade.

2

Participants turn right and follow the loop (counterclockwise) around the South Embarcadero Marina.

3

Turn right onto the Promenade toward the Hilton and make a U-turn before the hotel.

4

The return route follows the waterfront north toward Ruocco Park, loops around the park (counterclockwise), and continues back with a right turn to the start/finish area at the North Embarcadero Marina.
